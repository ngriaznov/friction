Regenerated by `cargo test -p friction-nlp --features nlprule --test perceptron_parity -- --nocapture`. Sentence pool: 260 sentences (fixtures.json accept/reject inputs, segment_golden.toml's 98 golden sentences, and the chatspeak/good_docs sample documents segmented with the real sentence segmenter).

| metric | agree | total | rate |
|---|---|---|---|

These are honest agreement numbers, not a gate: the perceptron is trained on a small hand-curated corpus (see `weights/NOTICE.md`) and is expected to disagree with nlprule on real cases, especially rarer tags. Nothing above is asserted by this test.
