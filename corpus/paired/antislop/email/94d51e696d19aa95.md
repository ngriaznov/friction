Subject: Incident Summary: Payment Processing API Outage - [Date] (2-Hour Impact)

Dear Engineering Team,

This email summarizes the recent incident impacting our payment processing API on [Date], resulting in a two-hour outage.

**Timeline:**

* **[Time of Start]:** Initial reports from monitoring and customer support indicated increased latency for transaction submissions.
* **[Time + 5 mins]:** On-call engineer, [Engineer Name], acknowledged the incident and initiated investigation. Automated dashboards confirmed connection pool exhaustion on the [Specific Service/Server] processing transactions.
* **[Time + 20 mins]:** Attempts to scale up the API servers were unsuccessful due to underlying resource constraints (connection limits).
* **[Time + 45 mins]:** Root cause identified as a misconfiguration introduced during yesterday’s release ([Release Version Number]) – specifically, a reduced connection pool limit in the [Configuration File/Location] that was unintentionally pushed to production.  The intended staging environment received the correct configuration.
* **[Time + 1 hour 15 mins]:** Configuration rollback deployed using [Deployment Tool/Method]. API functionality gradually restored as the application recovered connections.
* **[Time of End]:** Monitoring confirmed stable processing performance and incident declared resolved.

**Root Cause:** A newly introduced, overly restrictive connection pool limit on the database connection pool was inadvertently pushed to production during yesterday’s release. The configuration change did not properly validate against production capacity.

**Customer Impact:** Users experienced failed or delayed payment transactions, leading to frustration and potential loss of sales. Approximately [Number] attempted transactions were impacted (details in attached report).  Communication with critical customers ([Specific Customer Names/Segments]) was initiated by the Support team.

**Follow-Up Actions:**

* **Review & Improve Release Process:** Evaluate staging environment validation procedures for connection pool configurations. - Owner: [Release Engineer Name], Due Date: [Date – 1 week from today]
* **Automated Testing Enhancement:** Implement automated tests to specifically verify acceptable database connection limits during deployments. - Owner: [QA/Test Automation Lead], Due Date: [Date – 2 weeks from today]
* **Connection Pool Monitoring Improvements:** Enhance monitoring dashboards to proactively detect and alert on diminishing available connections in the pool. - Owner: [Monitoring Engineer Name], Due Date: [Date – 1 week from today]
* **Configuration Management Review:** Assess our configuration management practices to prevent unintentional deployment of incorrect configurations. -Owner: [Ops/Infrastructure Lead], Due Date: [Date – 3 weeks from today]

A post-incident review meeting is scheduled for [Date & Time] in [Location/Meeting Link]. Attendance is encouraged, particularly from those involved in the investigation and resolution efforts.

Sincerely,

[Incident Manager Name / Engineering Leadership]

**IMPORTANT NOTES (Please Read Before Sending):**

*   **Specificity:** Replace the bracketed placeholders with specific details relevant to your incident. The more precise you are, the better.
*   **Attachment:** Mention and attach a detailed report containing specifics about transactions failed/delayed.
*   **Customer Communication:** Ensure this aligns with communications already sent to customers if appropriate.
*   **Legal/Compliance:** Review for any legal or compliance requirements before distribution (especially around personally identifiable information).
*   **Sensitivity:** Adjust the level of technical detail based on your audience's familiarity. Sometimes, broader strokes are better.
*   **Positive Framing:** While honesty is crucial, focus on learning and improving, rather than blame assignment. The follow-up action items should be framed as opportunities for growth.
* **Timeline Accuracy:** Ensure all timestamps in the timeline are accurate to the best of your knowledge.
