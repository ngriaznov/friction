## Level Up Your Brewing: A Beginner’s Guide to Using Barmwatch

So you're taking the plunge into homebrewing? Awesome! Keeping track of your batches is crucial for consistent results and learning what works (and doesn't). That’s where apps like **Barmwatch** come in. It’s a powerful, yet intuitive tool that can dramatically improve your brewing process. This tutorial will walk you through the basics: logging a batch, setting temperature alerts, and exporting a professional-looking brew log to share or keep for future reference.

**What is Barmwatch?**

Barmwatch (available on iOS and Android) helps brewers monitor their beer batches during fermentation. It provides features for recording data, tracking gravity readings, predicting endpoints, and receiving notifications if temperatures stray outside your desired range. Think of it as a digital brewing journal packed with useful extras.

**Getting Started:**

1. **Download & Setup:** Download Barmwatch from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android).  Create an account – you can use email, Facebook, or Google login.
2. **The Interface:** The app’s landing screen displays a list of your active and archived batches.  At the bottom, you'll see three main icons: “+” for adding a new batch, "History" to view past batches, and a settings gear icon.

**Logging Your Batch – From Grain Bill to Gravity Readings:**

1. **Adding a New Batch (+ Button):** Tap the "+" button in the lower right corner.
2. **Batch Details - The Big Picture:** You'll start with "Batch Details." Here you can:
    * **Name Your Beer:** Give your batch a descriptive name (e.g., “Mango IPA,” “Classic Stout”).
    * **Recipe Origin:** Select whether this is your own, or taken from a published recipe.  If using a published recipe, there's often an opportunity to search for and import details directly into the app which will populate estimated values.
    * **Batch Size:** Enter the volume of beer you are creating (usually in gallons or liters).
    * **Expected ABV:** The app can estimate this based on your grain bill, but it’s a good idea to double-check.
3. **Ingredients (Optional):** Tap "Add Ingredient" and enter details about each ingredient – name, amount used, efficiency %.  While this isn't essential for tracking fermentation, it helps with more advanced recipe analysis later on.
4. **Fermentation Profile:** Specify your planned temperature range. This is *critical* because Barmwatch will use these settings to trigger alerts (see below).
5. **Save Batch:** Tap the checkmark in the top right corner.

**Setting Up Temperature Alerts – Your Brewing Guardian Angel:**

This is where Barmwatch shines.  You'll need a temperature probe and Bluetooth connection (compatible probes listed on their website) for this to work fully. 

1. **Connect Your Probe:** Ensure your Bluetooth probe is paired with your phone. The app will display the current temperature reported by the probe.
2. **Adjust Alert Ranges:** Return to your batch details screen. You’ll see a section labeled “Temperature.” Tap it.  You can now set:
    * **Low Temperature Alert:** The threshold that, when breached, triggers an alarm (e.g., 60°F for lager strains).
    * **High Temperature Alert:** The threshold that signals a potential overheating issue (e.g., 78°F for ale strains).
3. **Alert Settings:** Choose how you want to be notified - push notifications and/or audible alarms.

**Tracking Fermentation – Logging Those Vital Gravity Readings:**

1. **Taking Measurements:** After fermentation has started, regularly take hydrometer readings (Original Gravity - OG & Final Gravity - FG).
2. **Adding a Reading (+ Button on the Batch Screen):**  On your batch details screen, tap the "+" button labeled "Take Measurement."
3. **Enter Data:** Enter the date and gravity reading. The app automatically generates a fermentation chart showing your progress.
4. **Predicted End Gravity (FG):** Barmwatch will calculate an estimated FG based on your previous readings. This helps you decide when to move onto conditioning or bottling/kegging.

**Exporting Your Brew Log – Sharing Your Masterpiece:**

1. **Access Batch History:** Tap the "History" button in the lower navigation bar.
2. **Select Your Batch:** Choose the batch you want to export from the list.
3. **Export as PDF:**  Tap the three dots (more options) icon in the top right corner of the batch details screen, then choose “Export”. 
4. **Sharing Options:** You can now email the PDF directly or save it to your device for sharing later.

**Tips & Tricks:**

* **Explore Settings:** Take some time to explore the settings menu (gear icon). Customize units, notification preferences and other features.
* **Community Forums:** Check out the Barmwatch forums for brewing advice from experienced users and troubleshooting tips.



With a little practice, Barmwatch can become an invaluable tool in your brewing arsenal. Happy brewing!
