**Notes & Customization Guidance:**

*   **Audience Awareness:**  This email assumes a non-technical audience. Avoid jargon and explain technical terms simply (e.g., "SDK" is explained).
*   **Specificity:** Replace the bracketed placeholders with specific details relevant to your project. Vague language doesn’t inspire confidence.

*   **Legal Sign-Off:**  Be transparent about the legal hurdle. It's better to acknowledge it than have stakeholders discover the delay later. The “escalated” language shows you’re actively addressing it.
*   **Risk Assessment – Be Honest:** Don't sugarcoat risks. A realistic assessment builds trust. Provide a range for potential delays, not just an optimistic single number.
*   **Metrics - Show Early Results (Cautiously):** Sharing early metrics is good, but frame them as "initial observations" or "early trends." Avoid making definitive claims based on limited data.
*   **Tone:** Maintain a positive and proactive tone throughout the email. Focus on progress and solutions.
*   **Frequency & Distribution:** Adjust the frequency of these updates (weekly is common) and distribution list based on stakeholder preferences.
