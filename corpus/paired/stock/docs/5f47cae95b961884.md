## Level Up Your Brewing: A Beginner's Guide to Using Barmwatch

So you’re brewing your own beer – fantastic! Keeping track of that process is crucial for consistent results and learning what works best. That's where fermentation tracking apps come in, and today we'll explore **Barmwatch**, a popular choice for homebrewers of all levels. This tutorial will walk you through the basics: logging a batch, setting up temperature alerts, and exporting your brew log as a PDF.

**What is Barmwatch?**

Barmwatch (available on iOS and Android) is designed to monitor and record fermentation data. It allows you to track gravity readings, temperatures, and other important variables for each batch of beer you brew. Think of it as a digital brewing journal that sends alerts when things stray outside your desired ranges – a lifesaver for maintaining consistent temperature control!

**1. Setting Up Your Account & Creating a New Batch:**

* **Download & Sign-Up:** First, download Barmwatch from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android). Create an account – it’s free and allows you to back up your data.
* **Creating a New Batch:** Tap the "+" button on the main screen. This will start the "New Brew" wizard.
    * **Batch Name:** Give your batch a descriptive name (e.g., "IPA - First Attempt," "Belgian Wit").
    * **Beer Style:** Choose from Barmwatch's extensive list of beer styles or select “Custom” if your recipe doesn’t fit neatly into an existing category. This helps with data comparison later on.
    * **Recipe Details (Optional):** You can enter details like Original Gravity (OG), Final Gravity (FG) target, ABV (Alcohol By Volume) expectation, and boil volume. Don't worry too much about these initially – you can always adjust them later or skip this step entirely if you’re just getting started.
    * **Fermentation Vessel:** Choose the type of vessel you’re using (e.g., carboy, bucket, conical fermenter). This primarily affects temperature recommendations.

**2. Logging Data: Gravity Readings and Temperature are Key!**

The heart of Barmwatch is logging your data. Here's how:

* **Gravity Readings:**
    * Tap on the batch you created in the main screen. You’ll see a timeline.
    * Tap the "+" button labeled "Reading."
    * Select “Gravity” and enter your reading from your hydrometer or refractometer.  Don't forget to select the date and time!
    * Add any notes (e.g., "Slight haze," "Clean taste"). This is super helpful for future reference.
    * Tap "Save." Repeat this process every few days during fermentation.
* **Temperature Readings:**
    * Barmwatch will automatically log temperature readings if you have a compatible Bluetooth thermometer connected. If not, you'll need to manually enter them.
    *  Just like with gravity, tap the "+" button and choose “Temperature.”
    * Enter the current temperature of your fermenter. Again, date and time are important!
    * Add any notes (e.g., "Fridge failed briefly," "Room temp fluctuating").

**3. Setting Up Temperature Alerts – Your Fermentation Guardian:**

This is where Barmwatch *really* shines.  Temperature alerts prevent disasters by notifying you if your fermentation goes outside the ideal range for your chosen beer style.

* **Accessing Alert Settings:** Within your batch's screen, tap on "Alerts" at the bottom of the screen.
* **Setting High/Low Temperatures:** Barmwatch will suggest a temperature range based on the beer style you selected, but *always* double-check and adjust to match your specific recipe or preferences.  Enter your desired high and low temperatures.
* **Notification Method:** Choose how you want to be alerted – push notifications (most common), email, or both.

**4. Exporting Your Brew Log as a PDF:**

Once fermentation is complete (when gravity readings stabilize near your expected FG) it's time to document what happened!

* **Access the "Export" Menu:** From within your batch screen, tap on the three dots in the upper right corner and select “Export.”
* **Choose PDF Format:** Select "PDF" as the export format.
* **Naming & Saving:**  Give your PDF a descriptive name (e.g., "IPA - Batch 1 Log") and choose where to save it – email, cloud storage, or directly to your device.

**Tips for Beginners:**

* **Consistency is Key:** Try to take gravity readings at roughly the same time each day.
* **Experiment & Learn:** Don't be afraid to adjust settings and experiment with different alert ranges as you gain experience.
* **Barmwatch Community:**  Join the Barmwatch user community (often found on their website or social media) for tips, troubleshooting, and inspiration from other brewers!



With a little practice, Barmwatch will become an invaluable tool in your brewing arsenal, helping you brew better beer every time. Cheers to consistent results!
