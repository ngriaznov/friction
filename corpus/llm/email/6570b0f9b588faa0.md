Subject: Mobile Redesign — Weekly Update

Hi all,

Here's where things stand on the mobile app redesign this week.

**What shipped**

The biggest news this week is that the new onboarding flow is live for all new users as of Wednesday. This is the redesigned three-screen experience we walked through in the last stakeholder review — it replaces the old five-screen flow and gets people to their first meaningful action noticeably faster. Early numbers from the first two days look encouraging: completion rate is up compared to the old flow, though we want a full week of data before calling it a trend. We'll have a proper readout with numbers next week.

**What's blocked**

We hit a snag on the analytics side. The third-party analytics SDK we planned to integrate for the redesign (to give us better funnel visibility across the new flow) is currently stuck waiting on legal sign-off. Legal flagged some language in the vendor's data processing terms that needs clarification before we can move forward, and that back-and-forth is now in its second week. This doesn't affect anything currently live — the onboarding flow shipped without it — but it does mean we're flying a bit blind on some of the more granular funnel metrics we wanted for the next phase. I've asked legal for a status check and will flag if this looks likely to slip further.

**What's next**

Next week the team is focused on:

- Starting design work on the redesigned settings screen, which is the next major surface in scope
- A round of usability testing on the onboarding flow with five external participants, to catch anything the internal team missed
- Continuing to push on the analytics SDK approval in parallel

**Risks**

The one risk I want to flag clearly is App Store review timing. We're aiming to bundle the settings screen redesign into the next release, currently targeted for early next month. Apple's review times have been running longer than usual over the past few weeks — some teams we've talked to have seen five to seven days instead of the usual one to two. If that holds, it could push our release date out by up to a week. It's not a blocker yet, but I wanted this on your radar in case it affects any external communications or launch plans tied to this release. We'll know more once we submit and can give a firmer estimate at that point.

Happy to answer questions on any of this — feel free to reply here or grab time on my calendar.
