Subject: Mobile App Redesign — Weekly Status (Week of July 20)

Hi all,

Here's this week's update on the mobile app redesign. Short version: onboarding shipped, analytics is stuck behind legal, and we're still tracking to the current milestone but watching one schedule risk closely.

**What shipped this week**

The new onboarding flow went out to 100% of users on Tuesday. This is the three-screen flow replacing the old single-form signup — it walks new users through picking their notification preferences and connecting their first data source before dropping them into the home screen, rather than dumping everything on one page up front. Early numbers are encouraging: completion rate through all three screens is sitting at 78%, up from the 52% we were seeing complete the old single-page form. We'll have a fuller read on downstream retention in about two weeks once we have enough cohort data.

**What's blocked**

The third-party analytics SDK we planned to integrate this sprint is on hold pending legal sign-off. Legal flagged a clause in the vendor's data processing agreement around cross-border data transfer that needs review before we can ship anything using their SDK. We submitted the redline request last Thursday and are told to expect a response by early next week. In the meantime, engineering has scoped the integration work so it's ready to go the moment sign-off comes through — no engineering time is being lost to this, but the feature itself is on hold.

**What's planned for next week**

- Begin work on the redesigned profile and settings screens (the next item in the redesign sequence after onboarding)
- Finalize the analytics SDK integration, contingent on legal clearing it
- Start internal QA on the updated checkout flow ahead of its planned rollout the following week

**Risk: App Store review timeline**

We're planning to submit the build containing these changes for App Store review in about three weeks. Apple's stated review times have been running longer than usual over the past month — closer to 3-4 days on average versus the usual 24-48 hours — and we've also changed enough onboarding-adjacent UI that there's a nonzero chance of a manual review flag requiring a resubmission cycle. We're building a few days of buffer into the submission date to absorb this, but wanted to flag it now rather than have it be a surprise if the release date shifts by a few days.
