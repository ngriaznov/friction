Subject: Updated Parental Leave Policy — Effective August 1

Hi all,

I'm writing to let you know about a change to our parental leave policy that takes effect on August 1. HR has been reviewing this for the past quarter based on feedback from employees who've gone through the process over the last two years, and the update reflects that feedback directly.

**What's changing**

Paid parental leave is increasing from 10 weeks to 16 weeks for all employees welcoming a new child, whether through birth, adoption, or foster placement. This applies equally regardless of gender or which parent is designated as primary caregiver — we're moving away from the old primary/secondary caregiver distinction, which several of you told us felt outdated and, in some cases, created confusion about who qualified for what.

**Eligibility**

The 16 weeks is available to any employee who has completed 90 days of employment at the time leave begins, up from the previous 180-day requirement. Leave can be taken in a single continuous block or split into two blocks within the first 12 months after the child's arrival, with manager approval for the split option. Part-time employees are eligible on a pro-rated basis tied to their standard weekly hours.

**How this interacts with short-term disability**

For employees giving birth, short-term disability continues to run concurrently with the start of parental leave, covering the physical recovery period (typically 6-8 weeks depending on delivery type, as certified by your physician). Parental leave then continues beyond that point up to the full 16 weeks — it does not stack on top of the disability period. If you're planning a leave and want to understand exactly how the two periods will line up for your situation, that's a great question to bring to HR directly, since it can vary based on your specific medical timeline.

**What you need to do**

If you're not currently planning any leave, there's nothing to do — this is simply an update to the policy on file. If you are currently on leave or have a leave scheduled to begin before August 1, please reach out to HR so we can review whether the new terms apply to your situation; in most cases we'll be extending the new duration to anyone whose leave is active or begins within 30 days of the effective date.

**Questions**

For anything specific to your own circumstances — timing, how it interacts with an existing disability claim, or anything else — please schedule time directly with Priya in HR (priya.hr@company.com) rather than working through the general details in this email. She's set aside dedicated office hours every Tuesday and Thursday afternoon this month specifically for parental leave questions, and can also send a calendar invite outside those windows if neither time works.

We know this is a meaningful benefit and we're glad to be able to expand it. Thanks for your patience through the review process, and don't hesitate to reach out with questions.
