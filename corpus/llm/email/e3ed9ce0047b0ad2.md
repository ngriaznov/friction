**Subject:** Request: dedicated staging environment for checkout v2 end-to-end testing (needed by 14 Aug)

Hi DevOps team,

I'm asking for a dedicated staging environment so QA can run end-to-end tests against the new checkout flow before the release freeze. Details below — happy to walk through any of it in person.

**Why a dedicated environment**

The shared staging environment is currently being used by the catalog and search teams for their own integration work. Checkout v2 changes the order state machine and introduces a new payment-intent step, so our tests need to create, cancel, and refund orders repeatedly. On shared staging that both pollutes their data and makes our own results unreliable — we have already had three test runs invalidated by someone else's deploy landing mid-suite.

**Services required**

Ideally an isolated namespace containing:

- `checkout-api` (release branch build, not main)
- `order-service`
- `payment-orchestrator`
- `inventory-service`
- `notification-service` — can point at a mail catcher rather than a real SMTP relay
- Postgres instance, isolated, not shared with any other environment
- Redis for session and cart storage

External dependencies should be stubbed rather than live. Specifically we need the payment gateway pointed at its sandbox, and the tax calculation service pointed at the vendor's test tenant. We do not need the fraud-scoring vendor at all; a stub that always returns "approve" is fine, and I can supply the contract.

**Test data seeding**

We need a repeatable seed, not a one-time database copy. Our suite assumes:

- roughly 500 products across at least 12 categories, including 20 marked out of stock and 10 with a quantity limit
- 50 customer accounts with known credentials, of which 15 have saved payment methods and 10 have addresses in different tax jurisdictions
- 5 active promotional codes covering percentage, fixed-amount, and free-shipping cases

I have a seeding script that produces all of this and can commit it wherever you prefer. The important part is that we can re-run it on demand — realistically we will want to reset between full suite runs, so a "wipe and reseed" job we can trigger ourselves would save a lot of back-and-forth.

**Timeline**

The release freeze is 28 August. We need roughly two weeks of test execution before that, which puts our requested handover date at **14 August**. If that is not achievable, please tell me as early as you can and I will re-scope the suite to prioritise the payment-intent paths and drop the promotional-code matrix.

Thanks — let me know what you need from my side to get this moving.
