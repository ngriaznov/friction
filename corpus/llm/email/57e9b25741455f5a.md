**Subject:** CRM migration — status through Nov 14, cutover weekend confirmed

Hi all,

Two-week update on the Salesforce → HubSpot migration. Short version: data migration is ahead of where we expected, custom field mapping is the long pole, training is scheduled, and the cutover weekend is holding at Dec 6–8.

**Data migration**

We have completed test loads of the three largest objects into the HubSpot sandbox:

- **Contacts** — 214,000 of 218,400 records loaded. The 4,400 rejects are almost all missing or malformed email addresses on records last touched before 2019. Rather than fix them, I'd propose we archive them to a flat export and leave them out. Please push back by Nov 21 if any of your teams still work these.
- **Companies** — 31,200 records, clean load, no rejects. Dedupe against HubSpot's domain-matching logic collapsed about 900 records that were duplicates in Salesforce. We have the merge log if anyone wants to audit it.
- **Deals** — 12,600 open and closed opportunities loaded. Deal stages map cleanly for the standard pipeline. The Enterprise pipeline needed two stages combined (see below).

Activity history — emails, calls, notes — is the remaining piece. We're loading 24 months back rather than full history. Anything older stays queryable in the read-only Salesforce archive through the end of next year.

**Custom fields still being mapped**

Nine fields are unresolved. These need a decision from a field owner, not engineering work:

1. `Account_Health__c` — free-text in Salesforce, no consistent values. Proposal: retire it and use HubSpot's scoring property instead. *Owner: Priya*
2. `Renewal_Risk_Tier__c` — picklist of five values, but only three are in active use. Proposal: collapse to three. *Owner: Priya*
3. `Partner_Source__c` and `Referral_Partner__c` — overlapping, both populated inconsistently. Needs a decision on which survives. *Owner: Marcus*
4. `Contract_Signed_Date__c` vs. `Close_Date` — these disagree on about 8% of closed-won deals. Need a rule for which is authoritative for reporting. *Owner: Dana*
5. Enterprise pipeline stages "Legal Review" and "Procurement" — currently proposing we merge into a single "In Contracting" stage. *Owner: Marcus*
6. Three territory/ownership fields that were built for the old regional structure and may not need to carry over at all. *Owner: Dana*

I've sent each owner a short doc with the data profile behind their fields. **I need decisions by Nov 26** to build them into the production load.
