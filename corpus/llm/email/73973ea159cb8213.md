Three observations.

**Surgery and Oncology are behind, and the cause is scheduling rather than reluctance.** Both departments told us the lab sessions were released in blocks that collide with their heaviest clinical hours. We have added fourteen additional lab slots between 20 and 31 October, weighted toward 06:00–08:00 and 19:00–21:00, and opened two Saturday sessions on the 25th. Dr. Okonjo and Dr. Barrett, your schedulers have the block codes.

**The float pool is the real risk.** Float staff work across units that will be on different Meridian versions during the phased rollout, so they need both the current and upgraded workflows. We are building a 25-minute "working across versions" supplement; it will be assigned automatically on 18 October and is mandatory for anyone in the float pool before 1 November. Nurse managers, please expect the assignment notification and do not defer it.

**Physicians who completed training more than five weeks ago are showing measurable skill decay** in the pilot. We are not going to re-run full training for them. Instead, the at-elbow support staffing on each go-live day has been increased from 1 per 12 beds to 1 per 8 beds, which the pilot suggests is closer to what is actually needed on day one.

## 2. Pilot findings — 4 West and 4 East

The pilot went live 1 October. Both units have remained on the upgraded build continuously since; no rollback was required. Fifty-one issues were logged. Nine are closed, thirty-one are in development or configuration queues, and eleven are open questions requiring clinical decisions. The items below are the ones with operational consequence.

**Medication reconciliation loses the prior-to-admission source annotation.** When a home medication list is carried forward, the field recording where the information came from (patient report, pharmacy fill history, outside record) does not populate in 9.2. Pharmacy caught this on day two. Vendor has confirmed it as a defect, fix targeted for the 9.2.3 patch on 6 November. **Interim:** the source must be entered in the free-text comment on each reconciled medication. Pharmacy has issued a one-page instruction; please make sure your residents have seen it.

**Order sets with nested conditional logic collapse to a flat list.** Twenty-three of our 210 order sets are affected, most heavily in Critical Care and peri-op. Rebuilding each takes roughly four hours of informatics time. We have prioritized eleven high-volume sets for completion before the next wave; the remaining twelve will go live in their flattened form with a warning banner. **Decision needed:** Critical Care and Surgery, please confirm by 22 October which of your sets you consider unsafe to run flattened, and we will move them up the queue.

**Vitals charting takes more taps.** The 9.2 flowsheet requires an explicit save per column where 8.6 saved on navigation. Nursing on both pilot units reported this as the single largest daily irritant. It is configurable. We have changed it and will carry the changed configuration forward to all units.

**Result inbox routing for covering physicians is unreliable** when coverage is assigned through the scheduling system rather than entered directly in Meridian. Two results were delayed by more than six hours during the pilot. Neither caused patient harm; both were reviewed by Quality and are documented. This is our highest-priority open item and the rollout will not proceed past Wave 2 until it is resolved.

**Printing to unit-based label printers required per-workstation reconfiguration** — about twenty minutes each. This is tedious rather than dangerous, but it is why the desktop team now arrives on each unit 48 hours before go-live rather than the morning of.

Each go-live is preceded by a readiness review 72 hours prior. Department heads or a delegate must attend their own; the invitation will come from my office roughly two weeks out. A wave will be held if training completion for that unit is below 90% combined, or if any open issue rated high-severity remains unresolved. Holding a wave is a normal outcome, not a failure — please tell us early if you think yours should be held.

The command center opens at 06:00 on every go-live day and stays staffed for 72 hours: extension 4400, or the Meridian Support channel. Issues logged there are triaged within thirty minutes.
